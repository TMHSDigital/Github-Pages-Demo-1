Privacy

This site, and the free calculators on it, are built to collect as little as possible. In short: nothing you type leaves your device unless you choose to send it. Last updated October 5, 2026.

What the site collects

Nothing, through the site itself. It has no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies: its fonts, scripts and images all come from this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic details of each request when your browser loads a page, and may keep them in its logs for security and operations. The GitHub General Privacy Statement explains how GitHub handles that.

The calculators

  • Every calculation runs in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
  • Copy a link puts your numbers in the link itself, so anyone you send it to can see them.
  • Print or save as PDF happens on your device.

What is kept in your browser

A few things are kept in your browser's own storage, on your device. They are never sent to us:

  • your choice of light or dark theme, once you pick one;
  • months you save in the prime cost tracker;
  • dishes and drinks you save to your menu;
  • copies of the pages you have opened, so they work offline (only the site's own files, never what you type).

Saved months and dishes can be removed one by one or all at once with the buttons beside them. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Getting in touch

The message builder only drafts text. When you paste it into LinkedIn or send it by email, that service handles it under its own privacy terms, and we receive what you send.

Calculators on other sites

When one of these calculators is embedded on another website, that website's privacy terms apply to its own pages. The calculator itself still sends nothing.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if the site starts counting visits, this page will be updated first. Questions are welcome on LinkedIn.
