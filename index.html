About

Strategy built from the floor up

Hospitality is a business of details: the menu, the shift schedule, the prep list, the P&L. TM Hospitality Strategies connects those details to the bigger picture so every decision supports the guest experience and the bottom line.

The work is hands-on and practical. Expect clear priorities, simple tools and recommendations your team can actually run on a Tuesday night.

Approach

A simple process, no mystery

Four steps from first conversation to results that last.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Walk the operation, review the numbers and hear from the people who run it.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Find the few changes that will move results the most and rank them by effort and impact.

  3. 03

    Build

    Put the plan, tools and training in place, with your team involved at each step.

  4. 04

    Sustain

    Track the right measures and adjust, so the gains hold after the engagement ends.

Independent restaurants, small groups and hospitality businesses that want practical, owner-level strategy.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Both are possible. On-site time is the most valuable for operations work, and strategy work can be done remotely.

It depends on scope. Pricing is shared after the first conversation, so there are no surprises.
